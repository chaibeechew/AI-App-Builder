# LANERIQ AI Project Buyout License

**Terms Version:** LANERIQ-BUYOUT-LICENSE-v1-DRAFT  
**Certificate Version:** LANERIQ-BUYOUT-CERT-v1  
**Status:** DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE GENERAL SELF-SERVICE ACTIVATION  

> This document records the intended product terms for the LANERIQ AI project-specific Buyout License. It must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before LANERIQ AI enables general self-service contractual acceptance.

## 1. Project-specific license

A Buyout License applies to one identified eligible LANERIQ AI project and is recorded using a unique electronic License ID. It does not automatically apply to other projects owned by the same customer.

## 2. Existing prices

The existing Buyout License prices remain:

- Personal: **US$49**
- Business: **US$199**
- Enterprise: **US$499**

The applicable tier and payment/receipt reference must be recorded before a License is issued.

## 3. Eligibility

A Buyout License must be selected before the project is published.

The following are not eligible:

- Game projects; and
- the specific project that actually redeemed LANERIQ AI Encourage Creator support.

Other eligible non-Game projects owned by the same Creator are not disqualified merely because another project used Encourage Creator support.

## 4. Customer ownership

The customer retains ownership of the customer's project and original project materials, subject to third-party rights, open-source licenses and other applicable rights.

The Buyout License does not transfer ownership of LANERIQ AI's platform, infrastructure, internal services, provider accounts, secrets, private keys or unrelated proprietary technology.

## 5. Source-code access

An active Buyout License unlocks the project source-code access defined by the LANERIQ AI Buyout policy. Source access remains subject to security controls and does not include another customer's data or LANERIQ AI private platform credentials.

## 6. Future LANERIQ AI revenue share after Buyout

For the project covered by an active Buyout License, the intended LANERIQ AI future revenue-share rate after the effective Buyout is **0%**, subject to the final legally approved terms.

Revenue or obligations accrued before the effective Buyout, if any, are not automatically cancelled unless the final terms expressly say so.

## 7. Electronic certificate

After payment/receipt confirmation and successful License issuance, LANERIQ AI creates an electronic certificate containing at least:

- unique License ID;
- Project ID and project name snapshot;
- license tier;
- license fee and currency;
- issue date/time;
- status;
- certificate version; and
- terms version.

The customer's Dashboard copy is the product source of truth. LANERIQ AI may also send a transactional email copy. Failure or delay of the email does not invalidate an already-issued active Dashboard License record.

## 8. External migration

An active eligible Buyout License satisfies the Buyout commercial path for that licensed project. A project without an active Buyout License remains subject to the applicable Project Portability / Revenue Share Agreement requirements before full external migration is enabled.

## 9. Third-party components

Third-party services, open-source components, app-store accounts, domains, API accounts and non-transferable licenses remain subject to their own terms. A LANERIQ AI Buyout License cannot grant rights LANERIQ AI does not own.

## 10. Stamp duty and governmental charges

Unless applicable law mandatorily allocates a particular liability to another person, the **Buyout Customer is responsible for any stamp duty, adjudication, filing, payment and related governmental charge attributable to the Buyout License or its execution**.

Accordingly:

- LANERIQ AI does not automatically calculate, assess, adjudicate, file, stamp or pay stamp duty on the Customer's behalf;
- LANERIQ AI does not automatically deduct stamp duty from the Buyout fee or charge a guessed duty amount;
- the Customer is responsible for determining whether any stamping or filing is required and for completing any required process within the applicable legal framework;
- the Customer is responsible for obtaining its own tax or legal advice where needed;
- LANERIQ AI may preserve the License ID, Project ID, exact Buyout terms version/hash, execution timestamp and any later stamping evidence supplied by the responsible person; and
- where mandatory law assigns liability differently, that mandatory legal allocation prevails to the extent required.

Nothing in this section represents that every Buyout License is necessarily chargeable, exempt or subject to a particular stamp-duty rate. LANERIQ AI keeps stamp-duty handling **MANUAL REVIEW ONLY** unless a later separately approved legal/tax implementation changes that policy.

## 11. Legal review gate

Before general Production self-service activation, qualified legal counsel should confirm at minimum:

- LANERIQ contracting entity;
- governing law and dispute process;
- exact rights granted by the Buyout License;
- treatment of pre-Buyout accrued revenue-share obligations;
- transfer/assignment rules;
- tax and stamp-duty treatment, including the intended Customer cost allocation subject to mandatory law;
- refund/cancellation policy;
- electronic acceptance requirements; and
- interaction with the Project Portability Agreement and platform Terms of Service.
