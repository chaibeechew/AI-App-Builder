# LANERIQ AI Project Portability & 10% Revenue Share Agreement

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement establishes the intended terms under which the owner of a LANERIQ AI project may, after publication, continue developing the project on LANERIQ AI or migrate the project to external hosting, another development team, another platform or the owner's own infrastructure.

LANERIQ AI does not require a project to remain technically locked to the platform. For a published project that does **not** already have an active eligible Buyout License, the intended migration path requires the Project Owner to accept this project-specific 10% Revenue Share Agreement before the full external migration package is enabled.

A project with an already-active eligible Buyout License follows the Buyout terms instead and is not intended to incur this additional 10% migration revenue share for the same post-Buyout Project Software Revenue.

The specific project that redeemed LANERIQ AI Encourage Creator support is not eligible for Buyout and therefore, if externally migrated, must use the applicable approved Revenue Share Agreement path.

## 2. Parties

This Agreement is intended to be between:

1. **CHAI BEE CHEW**, a Malaysian individual currently operating under the **LANERIQ AI** name before incorporation ("LANERIQ AI"), or the valid successor LANERIQ contracting entity described below; and
2. the person or legal entity that owns the LANERIQ AI account and the specific project identified at acceptance (the "Project Owner").

The current private notice address and notice email for CHAI BEE CHEW / LANERIQ AI must be stored in the private execution record and may be inserted into the final signed copy after legal review. They must not be exposed merely because the source-code repository is public.

### 2.1 Future Malaysian Company / Corporate Novation

CHAI BEE CHEW intends that LANERIQ AI may later be operated through a company incorporated in Malaysia and controlled directly or indirectly by CHAI BEE CHEW (the "Successor Company").

Subject to applicable law and the final legally approved agreement, CHAI BEE CHEW may transfer the LANERIQ contracting position to the Successor Company through an appropriate accession, assignment or novation document and written/electronic notice to the Project Owner.

The intended commercial rule is that a valid transition from CHAI BEE CHEW personally to the Successor Company:

- does not increase or duplicate the agreed Revenue Share percentage;
- does not reset the Project Owner's obligations or accrued rights merely because LANERIQ incorporates;
- does not transfer ownership of the Project Owner's project to LANERIQ AI;
- preserves accrued rights and liabilities up to the effective novation date; and
- causes the Successor Company to assume the applicable future LANERIQ rights and obligations from the effective novation date, to the extent permitted by the final novation document and applicable law.

Until a valid novation/accession becomes effective, CHAI BEE CHEW remains the intended LANERIQ contracting party for this draft structure.

### 2.2 No Double Charge on Entity Change

The formation or substitution of a future LANERIQ company does not by itself create a second Revenue Share, restart the commercial term, or convert a 10% obligation into 20%.

## 3. Project Covered

This Agreement applies only to the project identified in the acceptance record, including:

- Project ID;
- Project name;
- Project Owner account ID;
- Agreement version;
- acceptance timestamp; and
- tamper-resistant audit/acceptance record.

A separate acceptance is required for each project that uses this Agreement to migrate outside LANERIQ AI.

## 4. Project Ownership

The Project Owner retains ownership of the Project Owner's project and original project materials, subject to applicable third-party rights, open-source licenses, provider terms and other rights that may apply to individual components.

Signing this Agreement does **not** transfer ownership of the Project Owner's business, customer relationships, trademarks, customer data, underlying goods/services or unrelated business assets to LANERIQ AI.

## 5. No Technical Platform Lock-In

After this Agreement has been validly accepted for an eligible project, LANERIQ AI will not use a technical lock solely to force the Project Owner to continue hosting or developing that project on LANERIQ AI.

Subject to technical, security, licensing, export-control and legal constraints, the Project Owner may:

- continue editing and publishing the project on LANERIQ AI;
- export or migrate the project to the Project Owner's own infrastructure;
- move the project to another hosting provider;
- provide the project to another developer or development team; or
- continue development outside LANERIQ AI.

There is no separate mandatory migration fee under this Agreement. Optional consulting, migration assistance, hosting, support or other separately purchased professional services may be charged separately if the Project Owner elects to buy them.

## 6. 10% Revenue Share

After external migration under this Agreement, the Project Owner agrees to pay LANERIQ AI **10% of Project Software Revenue** attributable to the migrated project.

This 10% path is intended for projects that do not have an already-active eligible Buyout License. An active eligible Buyout License is intended to carry the Buyout policy's post-Buyout LANERIQ revenue-share rate instead.

### 6.1 Project Software Revenue

"Project Software Revenue" means amounts actually received by the Project Owner or a controlled project entity that are directly attributable to monetizing access to, use of, licensing of, subscription to, sale of, paid digital functionality of or commercial distribution of the migrated software project.

Examples include:

- paid app sales;
- SaaS or software subscription fees;
- paid digital features, upgrades or memberships;
- software licensing fees;
- paid access to the software's functionality; and
- other software monetization directly attributable to the migrated project.

### 6.2 Excluded Revenue

Unless a later signed agreement expressly states otherwise, Project Software Revenue does **not** include:

- sales, service or similar taxes collected for a government authority;
- refunds, reversals, chargebacks and fraudulent transactions that are actually reversed;
- amounts never received by the Project Owner because a payment processor or marketplace withheld or reversed the transaction;
- unrelated revenue from the Project Owner's other products or services;
- the value of physical property, real estate, vehicles, food, travel, healthcare, professional services or other offline goods/services merely advertised, booked, ordered, facilitated or managed through the software;
- customer money held in trust or on behalf of third parties;
- loans, grants, investments or capital contributions that are not payment for the project software; and
- other revenue that is not directly attributable to monetization of the migrated software itself.

### 6.3 No Double LANERIQ Share

The same unit of Project Software Revenue will not be charged two overlapping LANERIQ AI revenue-share percentages for the same commercial event unless the Project Owner separately agrees in writing.

If another LANERIQ AI commercial program already applies to the same Project Software Revenue, the final Production policy must specify which rate controls or how one payment is credited against the other.

An active eligible Buyout License must not be silently combined with this 10% migration share for the same post-Buyout revenue.

## 7. Duration of the 10% Revenue Share

**Product default for legal review:** the 10% Revenue Share continues for the commercial life of the migrated project, including renamed, upgraded or successor versions that are substantially derived from the same migrated project.

Before Production enforcement, qualified legal counsel must confirm or revise this duration and define when a successor project is sufficiently independent that it is no longer covered.

## 8. Revenue Reporting

Subject to final legal review, the intended operating model is:

- reporting once per calendar quarter;
- the report shows gross Project Software Revenue, permitted exclusions, net shareable Project Software Revenue and the resulting 10% LANERIQ amount;
- a zero-revenue quarter may be reported as zero; and
- the Project Owner keeps relevant supporting records for at least three years after the applicable reporting period, or for another period required by the final legally approved agreement.

LANERIQ AI does not receive permission under this Agreement to inspect unrelated business records or unrelated customer data.

## 9. Payment Timing and Currency

**Product default for legal review:** amounts due to LANERIQ AI are payable within 30 days after the end of each quarterly reporting period.

Before Production enforcement, the final agreement must specify:

- accepted payment currency or currencies;
- supported payment method;
- foreign-exchange treatment;
- applicable taxes and withholding;
- treatment of disputed amounts; and
- whether a minimum collection threshold applies.

The application must not invent or silently change these terms.

## 10. Verification and Audit

Subject to final legal review, the Project Owner will maintain records reasonably sufficient to verify Project Software Revenue.

Any audit right must be:

- limited to records relevant to the migrated project's software revenue;
- exercised on reasonable prior notice;
- no more frequent than once per calendar year unless fraud or material under-reporting is reasonably suspected;
- conducted in a way that minimizes disruption and protects confidential information; and
- subject to applicable privacy and data-protection law.

LANERIQ AI will not treat this Agreement as authorization to access unrelated private business data.

## 11. Migration Package

After the Agreement is validly accepted and the relevant project passes applicable security and export checks, LANERIQ AI may enable a project migration/export package. An already-active eligible Buyout License may satisfy the alternative commercial migration path according to the final approved product terms.

The migration process must clearly distinguish:

- Project Owner-owned source/materials that are exportable;
- LANERIQ AI proprietary platform services that are not transferred merely by exporting a project;
- third-party components and their licenses;
- secrets, private keys and credentials that must not be embedded in export bundles; and
- provider-specific services for which the Project Owner must configure a replacement account, credential or provider after migration.

LANERIQ AI should provide reasonable documentation describing the exported project structure and any known replacement requirements.

## 12. Continuing to Use LANERIQ AI

Signing this Agreement does not require immediate migration. The Project Owner may continue using LANERIQ AI after signing, subject to the Project Owner's applicable access plan and other platform terms.

The Project Owner may also migrate first and later return to LANERIQ AI if technically supported and mutually permitted by the then-current product terms.

## 13. Project Owner Transfer to a Company or Successor

A Project Owner should not be able to erase an otherwise applicable project-specific obligation merely by transferring the migrated project to a company, affiliate, successor or controlled entity.

Subject to final legal review, if the Project Owner transfers substantially all rights in the migrated project to another entity, the transfer should require an appropriate assumption/accession so that the project-specific obligations that lawfully survive transfer continue with the receiving entity. This provision must not create duplicate payment obligations for the same Project Software Revenue.

## 14. Confidentiality, Privacy and Data Protection

Each party must handle confidential and personal information in accordance with applicable law and any separately applicable privacy/data-processing terms.

Project migration must not expose another user's data, LANERIQ AI secrets, internal service credentials, unrelated tenant data or confidential infrastructure information.

## 15. Third-Party Services and Open-Source Components

The Project Owner remains responsible for complying with third-party service terms and open-source licenses applicable to components used in the project after migration.

A LANERIQ AI export or migration does not automatically transfer third-party subscriptions, API accounts, app-store accounts, domains, marketplace rights, licenses or credentials that cannot legally or technically be transferred.

## 16. Taxes

Each party is responsible for its own taxes as required by applicable law. The final Production agreement must specify how sales taxes, withholding taxes, VAT/GST/SST and similar amounts affect the calculation and payment of the Revenue Share where legally required.

## 17. Suspension for Material Breach

The final legally approved agreement may permit LANERIQ AI to suspend migration-related support or pursue contractual remedies for material breach, fraud or intentional under-reporting.

LANERIQ AI must not remotely disable or seize an externally migrated project merely because a payment dispute exists unless such action is expressly lawful, technically appropriate, contractually agreed and legally reviewed.

## 18. Termination and Survival

Termination of the Project Owner's LANERIQ AI account does not automatically cancel obligations that the final approved Agreement expressly states survive termination, including accrued payment obligations and any continuing Revenue Share term.

The final Agreement must specify the exact survival provisions.

## 19. Changes to This Agreement

LANERIQ AI may publish a future agreement version, but a material change to an already migrated project's Revenue Share obligations must not be silently imposed retroactively.

Where legally required or where the commercial terms materially change, LANERIQ AI must obtain a new affirmative acceptance from the Project Owner.

The acceptance record must retain the exact agreement version that the Project Owner accepted.
