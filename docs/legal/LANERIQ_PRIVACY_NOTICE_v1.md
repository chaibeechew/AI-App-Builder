# LANERIQ AI Privacy Notice

**Version:** LANERIQ-PRIVACY-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN PRIVACY/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION ACTIVATION  
**Controller / Operator:** the individual identified in the private legal execution record, trading publicly as **LANERIQ AI**, until a lawful successor company becomes the controller/operator.

This Notice describes the intended handling of personal data in LANERIQ AI. It must be aligned with the actual Production data map before activation.

## 1. Scope

This Notice applies to account creation, authentication, project creation, AI generation, support, payments, publishing assistance, security, marketplace transactions, project transfers and related LANERIQ AI services.

A project creator may separately act as controller of personal data collected through the app or website the creator builds. LANERIQ AI does not automatically become controller of every creator's end-user data merely because the project was generated using LANERIQ AI.

## 2. Data categories

Depending on the feature used, LANERIQ AI may process:

- account identifiers, name, email and authentication records;
- plan, purchase, invoice and payment-status information;
- project prompts, specifications, files and generated artifacts;
- usage, feature, device, browser, network and diagnostic information;
- security, fraud, malware and abuse-detection events;
- publishing metadata and customer-provided store declarations;
- marketplace listing, transaction, contract and acceptance records;
- customer-support communications;
- consent, Terms Version and electronic-signature evidence;
- limited technical metadata necessary for local/device-assisted processing; and
- other information a user intentionally provides.

Government ID numbers, signature images, banking credentials and other high-risk identity evidence must not be stored in the public GitHub repository. If legally required for KYC, payment, e-signature or dispute handling, such information must use a separate private process with access controls and retention limits.

## 3. Purposes

Personal data may be processed as reasonably necessary to:

1. provide, secure and administer the service;
2. authenticate users and protect accounts;
3. generate, build, test, store and publish user-requested projects;
4. operate subscriptions, purchases, Buyout, portability and marketplace workflows;
5. maintain transaction and legal evidence;
6. prevent fraud, malware, abuse, unauthorized access and policy violations;
7. respond to support and legal requests;
8. improve reliability, quality, accessibility and safety using privacy-respecting analytics;
9. comply with legal, tax, accounting, security and regulatory obligations; and
10. establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

User content must not be silently repurposed for unrelated model training or unrelated commercial exploitation. Any such use requires a separate lawful basis, clear disclosure and appropriate controls.

## 4. Legal basis / permitted processing

The final Production Notice must map each category and purpose to the applicable basis or permitted ground under Malaysian law and, where relevant, other jurisdictions. Depending on context this may include performance of a contract, user consent, legal obligation, legitimate business/security purposes where recognized, or other lawful grounds.

Where consent is relied upon, the system should make it specific enough for the relevant purpose and record withdrawal where legally required.

## 5. AI and service providers

LANERIQ AI may use third-party providers for AI inference, hosting, databases, storage, authentication, payments, analytics, security, email, app-store related services and other infrastructure.

Only data reasonably necessary for the requested function should be disclosed to a provider, subject to provider configuration, contract, security controls and the Provider Router architecture.

Provider availability, location and sub-processors may change. The Production privacy page must maintain an accurate current processor/provider disclosure appropriate to the service.

## 6. Device-first / local processing

Where LANERIQ AI offers local or device-assisted computation, the product must clearly disclose the relevant device permissions and resource use. Local processing does not eliminate privacy obligations for data that is subsequently transmitted to LANERIQ AI or another provider.

Consent to ordinary Terms must not be treated as blanket consent to undisclosed intensive device use, unrelated background computation or collection of unrelated device data.

## 7. Marketplace and project transfers

Selling source code does not automatically authorize transfer of customer or end-user personal data.

Where an App Sale includes personal data, the parties must complete the LANERIQ App Sale Data Transfer Addendum and identify, as applicable:

- the dataset and categories transferred;
- seller and buyer roles;
- permitted purpose;
- legal basis, notice and consent requirements;
- retention/deletion requirements;
- security controls;
- breach responsibilities;
- international-transfer controls; and
- post-completion access revocation.

LANERIQ AI may block a platform-assisted data export when required declarations are missing or a serious legal/security risk is detected.

## 8. Disclosure

LANERIQ AI may disclose personal data only as reasonably necessary to:

- contracted service providers and processors;
- payment providers and financial institutions for transaction processing;
- app stores or publishing providers when the user instructs publication;
- professional advisers bound by appropriate confidentiality duties;
- authorities where lawfully required; or
- a lawful successor operator as part of a properly executed business transition, subject to required notice/consent and data-protection obligations.

LANERIQ AI must not sell personal data to advertisers merely because the user uses the service.

## 9. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred or made accessible outside Malaysia, LANERIQ AI must apply the then-applicable Malaysian cross-border transfer requirements and other applicable laws, including appropriate contractual, organizational and technical safeguards where required.

The final Production policy must be aligned to the actual provider/data-location map.

## 10. Retention

Personal data should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, contractual obligations, security, fraud prevention, backups, dispute handling, legal claims or legal/regulatory retention requirements.

The Production implementation should define category-specific retention periods rather than relying on indefinite retention.

Deletion of a user account does not necessarily require immediate deletion of records that LANERIQ AI must lawfully preserve for payment, tax, fraud, security, signed contracts, transaction evidence or legal claims.

## 11. Security

LANERIQ AI should apply reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures proportionate to risk, including access controls, least privilege, credential isolation, encryption where appropriate, logging, malware/security checks, secrets management, backup controls and incident response.

No system is perfectly secure. Users must also protect their credentials and devices.

## 12. Data breaches and incidents

LANERIQ AI must maintain an incident-response process capable of assessing whether a personal-data breach triggers notification to the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Commissioner, affected data subjects or other authorities under applicable law.

The final Production process must reflect the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended, applicable commencement orders, regulations, circulars and guidance in force at the time of the incident.

## 13. Data Protection Officer and registration

LANERIQ AI must periodically assess whether applicable Malaysian law, regulations, orders or Commissioner guidance requires appointment of a Data Protection Officer, registration as a data controller, or other sector/class-specific obligations.

The approximately 1,000-user company-transition trigger does not replace any earlier legal requirement that may arise based on processing activity, sector, scale or risk.

## 14. User rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to request access, correction, withdrawal of consent where relevant, restriction of certain processing, information about processing, complaint mechanisms and other statutory rights.

The final Production page must describe the actual rights and request channels available under then-current law and must not promise rights the platform cannot lawfully or technically fulfil.

## 15. Children and age-sensitive use

LANERIQ AI should not knowingly process children's personal data in a manner inconsistent with applicable law. Features directed to minors or likely to process children's data require additional product and legal review before activation.

## 16. Automated systems

LANERIQ AI uses automated and AI systems to generate content, route providers, identify security risk, test software and assist workflows. Where automated processing produces material consequences for a user, the product should provide meaningful review, correction or appeal mechanisms where legally required or appropriate to risk.

## 17. Cookies and similar technologies

The Production implementation must maintain an accurate cookie/storage inventory. Non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technologies must not be described as strictly necessary and should use consent controls where required by applicable law.

## 18. Operator transition

Until a lawful successor-company transition is effective, the current individual operator remains the controller/operator identified in the private legal record.

A successor company may assume the relevant role only after incorporation, legal review, appropriate transfer/novation/assignment arrangements, privacy due diligence and legally sufficient notice or consent where required.

## 19. Contact and complaints

The final Production Notice must publish a business-appropriate privacy contact channel without exposing private residential information or unnecessary identity documents.

Users must be informed of any applicable right to complain to the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Commissioner or another competent authority.

## 20. Legal review gate

Before activation, qualified Malaysian counsel/privacy professionals should verify this Notice against the actual Production data flows, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and 2024 amendments, commencement orders, breach-notification rules, DPO/registration requirements, cross-border transfer rules, sector obligations, processor contracts and actual third-party providers.
