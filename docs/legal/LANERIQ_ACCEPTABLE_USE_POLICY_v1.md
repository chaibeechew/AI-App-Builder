# LANERIQ AI Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** LANERIQ-AUP-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION ACTIVATION

This Policy protects LANERIQ AI, its users, third parties and infrastructure from misuse. It supplements the Platform Terms of Service.

## 1. General rule

Users may use LANERIQ AI for lawful creation, development, testing, publishing and commercial activity, subject to these restrictions and applicable law.

## 2. Prohibited security abuse

Users must not use LANERIQ AI to:

- deploy malware, ransomware, credential theft, destructive payloads or malicious backdoors;
- gain or attempt unauthorized access to accounts, devices, networks, databases or services;
- bypass authentication, authorization, rate limits, security gates or platform safeguards;
- perform denial-of-service attacks or intentionally exhaust shared infrastructure;
- steal secrets, private keys, session tokens or privileged credentials;
- distribute spyware or conduct covert surveillance without lawful authorization and required notice/consent;
- conceal malicious functionality inside generated apps or code; or
- use platform features to materially facilitate cybercrime or fraud.

Security research must stay within expressly authorized systems and scope.

## 3. Fraud and deception

Users must not use the service for impersonation, payment fraud, fake investment schemes, fraudulent marketplaces, phishing, fabricated ownership claims, knowingly misleading transaction evidence or other deceptive commercial practices.

## 4. Intellectual property

Users must not knowingly upload, generate, sell or transfer material they lack authority to use for the intended purpose.

Users selling a project must disclose known material third-party and open-source restrictions. LANERIQ AI may pause a transfer after a credible ownership or infringement complaint while preserving relevant evidence.

## 5. Privacy and personal data

Users must not unlawfully collect, sell, export, disclose or transfer personal data through LANERIQ AI.

A project sale does not authorize automatic transfer of the project's user database. Required privacy assessments and the App Sale Data Transfer Addendum must be completed before LANERIQ-assisted personal-data transfer.

## 6. Harmful or unlawful content

Users must not use LANERIQ AI to create, publish or distribute content or services whose primary purpose is unlawful conduct, exploitation, credible threats, targeted harassment, illegal discrimination or other conduct prohibited by applicable law.

## 7. Platform integrity

Users must not:

- resell or share account access contrary to the applicable plan;
- automate abusive high-volume usage designed primarily to consume resources rather than perform legitimate work;
- misrepresent browser emulation, synthetic testing or provider readiness as verified real-world approval;
- interfere with logs, evidence, audit records or security controls;
- exploit bugs to obtain unpaid services or another user's data; or
- use multiple accounts to evade an active enforcement measure.

## 8. Marketplace integrity

A marketplace Seller must not knowingly:

- list assets the Seller lacks authority to sell;
- falsify revenue, traffic, user counts or app-store status;
- conceal material malware, known ownership disputes or non-transferable dependencies;
- transfer credentials that expose another customer or LANERIQ AI infrastructure; or
- transfer personal data without required legal authority.

A Buyer must not use due-diligence access to copy excluded assets, steal credentials, scrape unrelated data or evade the transaction process.

## 9. Enforcement

LANERIQ AI may use proportionate measures including warnings, feature limits, rate limits, transfer holds, content removal, credential reset requirements, temporary suspension or termination.

Immediate restriction may be used for serious fraud, malware, account compromise, imminent security risk, unlawful data transfer or credible threats to platform integrity.

Where reasonably practicable, non-urgent enforcement should identify the reason and provide a route to correct or appeal the issue.

## 10. Preservation and disclosure

LANERIQ AI may preserve relevant records where reasonably necessary for security investigations, payment disputes, IP disputes, signed transactions, legal claims or lawful requests.

Information will be disclosed to authorities or third parties only as permitted or required by law and the Privacy Notice.

## 11. No waiver of lawful use

This Policy is not intended to prohibit legitimate security testing, accessibility work, defensive research, journalism, education, interoperability, migration or other lawful activity merely because it involves technically sensitive subject matter.

## 12. Legal review gate

Before Production activation, qualified counsel should review this Policy together with the Platform Terms, Privacy Notice, marketplace rules and actual enforcement system to ensure restrictions are clear, proportionate and consistent with applicable law.
