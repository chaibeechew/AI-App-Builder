# LANERIQ AI Enterprise Data Processing Addendum

**Version:** LANERIQ-DPA-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN LEGAL/PRIVACY REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE CONTRACTUAL ACTIVATION

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is intended for Business/Enterprise customers where LANERIQ AI processes personal data on behalf of the customer in connection with the services.

## 1. Roles

The final order form or execution record must identify, for each relevant processing activity, whether the customer acts as data controller and LANERIQ AI acts as processor, whether LANERIQ AI acts as an independent controller for a limited purpose, or another lawful allocation applies.

No label in this draft overrides the parties' actual legal roles.

## 2. Documented instructions

LANERIQ AI will process customer personal data only on documented customer instructions and as necessary to provide, secure and support the contracted services, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

LANERIQ AI may refuse an instruction that appears unlawful, technically unsafe, outside the contracted scope, or likely to expose another customer's data or LANERIQ AI secrets.

## 3. Confidentiality

Personnel and authorized service providers with access to customer personal data must be bound by appropriate confidentiality duties and access controls.

## 4. Security

LANERIQ AI should maintain risk-appropriate technical and organizational measures including, as applicable: authentication, least privilege, encryption in transit, secret isolation, environment separation, logging, vulnerability management, backups/recovery, incident response and secure deletion workflows.

No DPA statement may be used to claim a security certification or control that has not actually been verified.

## 5. Subprocessors

LANERIQ AI may use subprocessors for hosting, AI/model processing, communications, analytics, monitoring, payments or support. The final DPA must define notice/objection mechanics appropriate to the service and must not promise veto rights that cannot operationally be honored.

LANERIQ AI remains responsible for imposing appropriate contractual data-protection obligations on subprocessors to the extent required by applicable law.

## 6. International transfers

Cross-border processing or access must follow applicable Malaysian data-protection requirements and any additional transfer mechanism contractually agreed with the customer. The service must not promise data residency in a location unless the actual provider routing and storage architecture supports it.

## 7. Data subject requests

Taking into account the nature of processing, LANERIQ AI will provide commercially reasonable assistance for access, correction, deletion or other legally applicable rights requests where the customer cannot reasonably fulfill the request without LANERIQ AI assistance.

## 8. Personal-data breach

LANERIQ AI will maintain an incident process and notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting customer data where notification is required by the DPA or applicable law. The final notice SLA, content, regulatory allocation and contact channel require legal and operational approval.

A security alert that is still unverified must not automatically be represented as a confirmed personal-data breach.

## 9. Deletion and return

At the end of the relevant service, LANERIQ AI will return or delete customer personal data according to the contracted retention/deletion process, subject to backups, legal holds, fraud/security evidence, tax/accounting records and other lawful retention requirements.

## 10. Audit and evidence

Enterprise customers may receive reasonable compliance evidence appropriate to the service. Audit rights must be proportionate, protect other customers and LANERIQ AI confidential/security information, and may use independent reports or structured questionnaires where suitable.

No customer may use an audit right to obtain another customer's data, LANERIQ private keys, exploit details, private system prompts or unnecessary source code.

## 11. Customer responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for lawful collection, notices/consents where required, documented instructions, user authorization, data accuracy, permissions and the legality of the customer's application use cases.

## 12. Order of precedence

Once legally approved, this DPA should prevail over conflicting general privacy language only for the specific processing obligations within its scope. The main agreement governs commercial matters not specifically modified here.

## 13. Legal review gate

Before activation, Malaysian counsel/privacy professionals must confirm the final controller/processor terminology and obligations under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended, breach notification, DPO requirements, transfer rules, subprocessor terms, retention, audit and any sector-specific requirements.
