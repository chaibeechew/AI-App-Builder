# LANERIQ AI App Sale — Personal Data Transfer Addendum

**Version:** LANERIQ-APP-SALE-DATA-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — PRIVACY/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

This Addendum applies only when an App Sale involves personal data, user accounts, customer records, analytics identifiers, communications or other information regulated as personal data.

## 1. No automatic database transfer

Sale of source code does not automatically include personal data. Personal data export/transfer remains blocked until the required declarations and security steps are complete.

## 2. Seller declaration

Seller must identify:

- categories of personal data involved;
- approximate number of affected users/records;
- purposes for which the data was collected;
- relevant privacy notices/consents;
- retention commitments;
- sensitive/special-risk categories, if any;
- current hosting/storage locations;
- current processors/subprocessors; and
- any known unresolved privacy or security incidents.

## 3. Buyer declaration

Buyer must identify:

- lawful intended purpose after acquisition;
- intended controller/business identity;
- privacy notice destination;
- security controls and access restrictions;
- retention/deletion plan;
- processor/subprocessor changes;
- cross-border storage/transfer plan; and
- contact method for data-subject requests.

## 4. Mandatory transfer gate

LANERIQ AI may refuse or delay platform-assisted personal-data transfer where:

- legally required notice/consent or other lawful basis has not been established;
- ownership/control of the App is disputed;
- Buyer identity is not sufficiently verified;
- a material breach/security incident remains unresolved;
- transfer would expose credentials or another customer's data;
- the requested export materially exceeds the declared transaction scope; or
- applicable law, regulator direction or court order restricts the transfer.

## 5. Data minimisation

Only data reasonably necessary for the lawful continuation of the acquired App should be transferred. Seller should delete or retain separate data only according to applicable law and documented legal obligations.

## 6. Security

Personal data must not be delivered through public repositories, public links, screenshots, unencrypted email attachments or hard-coded application secrets.

Where feasible, transfers should use encrypted channels, least-privilege access, time-limited credentials and auditable export/import events.

## 7. Credentials

Passwords, API secrets, private keys and provider credentials are not personal-data assets and should not be copied as part of the data transfer. Buyer should establish new credentials and Seller should revoke/rotate old credentials after verified handover.

## 8. Breach responsibility

Each party remains responsible for breaches, unlawful processing or failures attributable to that party's period of control, subject to applicable law and the main App Sale Agreement.

## 9. LANERIQ AI role

LANERIQ AI may provide technical transfer tooling and evidence records but does not warrant that Seller's or Buyer's privacy declarations are legally sufficient. The parties remain responsible for obtaining professional advice where required.

## 10. Execution

Where this Addendum is required, Seller and Buyer must electronically sign it under the LANERIQ AI Electronic Signature & Acceptance Evidence Standard before personal-data export is enabled.
