# LANERIQ AI Cookie & Tracking Notice

**Version:** LANERIQ-COOKIE-TRACKING-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN LEGAL/PRIVACY REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION ENFORCEMENT  

This Notice is intended to explain how LANERIQ AI may use cookies, local storage, SDK identifiers and similar technologies on websites, web apps and supported applications.

## 1. Core rule

LANERIQ AI should use the minimum tracking needed for security, authentication, reliability, user-requested features and lawful analytics. Advertising or non-essential tracking must not be silently treated as necessary.

## 2. Categories

### Strictly necessary
May be used where required for login, session continuity, CSRF/security controls, fraud prevention, load balancing, preferences required for the requested service, and transaction integrity.

### Functional
May remember optional preferences such as language, layout or recently selected workspace where appropriate.

### Analytics
May measure aggregate product usage, crashes, performance, feature adoption and conversion. Analytics should be configured to minimize collection of raw identifiers and free-form user content where feasible.

### Advertising / cross-context tracking
Must remain disabled unless expressly approved through a separate legal, privacy and product review. LANERIQ AI must not infer that account creation alone is consent to targeted advertising tracking.

## 3. Consent and controls

Where consent is legally required, the product should provide a clear choice before activating the relevant non-essential technology. Refusal of non-essential tracking must not block core service functionality unless that technology is genuinely necessary for the requested feature.

Consent records should include the notice version, categories accepted or refused, timestamp and account/session evidence sufficient to demonstrate the choice.

Users should be able to revisit tracking preferences without deleting their account.

## 4. Local-first and device storage

LANERIQ AI may use device-local storage for local-first functionality, drafts, project state, cached assets and preferences. Device-local storage is not automatically the same as advertising tracking. The product should distinguish functional local storage from analytics or marketing identifiers.

## 5. Third parties

Third-party providers may set or read identifiers only within the scope approved for the relevant service. Provider SDKs must not be assumed to be privacy-neutral. Before activation, LANERIQ AI should document purpose, data categories, retention, access, transfers and opt-out/consent behavior.

## 6. Sensitive content

Raw prompts, source code, project secrets, private keys, authentication tokens, payment data and sensitive personal data should not be intentionally placed into analytics or advertising payloads unless specifically required, legally justified and separately protected.

## 7. Children and high-risk users

Tracking for children, education/minor contexts or other specially regulated audiences requires separate legal review and age-appropriate safeguards before activation.

## 8. Change management

Material expansion of tracking purposes or introduction of advertising/cross-site tracking requires a new notice version and reassessment of whether fresh consent is required.

## 9. Relationship to Privacy Notice

This Notice supplements the LANERIQ AI Privacy Notice. If tracking data constitutes personal data, the Privacy Notice and applicable data-protection requirements also apply.

## 10. Legal review gate

Before Production activation, qualified Malaysian counsel/privacy professionals should confirm the final consent model, categories, retention, cross-border transfers, provider SDK behavior and interaction with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended and any applicable guidance or regulations.
