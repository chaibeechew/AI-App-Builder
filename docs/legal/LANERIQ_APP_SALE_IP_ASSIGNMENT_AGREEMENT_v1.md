# LANERIQ AI App Sale & Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

**Agreement Version:** LANERIQ-APP-SALE-IP-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE GENERAL SELF-SERVICE ACTIVATION  
**Intended governing law:** Malaysia, subject to final legal review and mandatory law.

This Agreement is intended for a transaction in which a Creator/Seller sells an identified software project or App to a Buyer through or with assistance from LANERIQ AI.

## 1. Parties and transaction record

The final execution record must identify:

- Seller / Assignor;
- Buyer / Assignee;
- LANERIQ AI Operator: the current individual operator identified in the private contracting-party record, trading publicly as **LANERIQ AI**, unless a lawful successor company has completed the transition described in Section 18;
- immutable LANERIQ AI Project ID;
- project name snapshot;
- sale price and currency;
- payment reference;
- Agreement Version;
- signing timestamps; and
- completion status.

The private legal name, address, identity/KYC records, signature specimen and personal notice details of the LANERIQ AI Operator must not be committed to the public repository.

## 2. LANERIQ AI role

Unless a separate signed document expressly states otherwise, LANERIQ AI and its Operator:

1. provide platform software, transaction workflow, evidence records, generation/build tools and transfer-assistance features;
2. are not the Seller or Buyer of the Project;
3. are not a fiduciary, broker, investment adviser, legal adviser, tax adviser or guarantor;
4. do not guarantee profitability, valuation, future revenue, future app-store approval, continuous provider availability or absence of all defects; and
5. do not independently certify every ownership claim or third-party licence unless an express verification service says so.

LANERIQ AI is not an escrow provider or trustee unless a separately approved regulated escrow/payment arrangement is activated.

## 3. Sale and assignment

Subject to Completion under Section 6, the Seller sells and assigns to the Buyer only the assets expressly listed as **Transferred Assets** in the signed Asset Schedule.

No ownership transfer occurs merely because the Buyer views a preview, receives demo access, pays a deposit, begins due diligence or signs an incomplete record.

To the maximum extent legally permitted, title passes only after all Completion Conditions are satisfied and the transaction is recorded as **COMPLETED**.

## 4. Transferred Assets

Transferred Assets may include only when specifically listed:

- Seller-owned source code and object code;
- Seller-owned UI/UX designs and project-specific visual assets;
- project-specific documentation;
- Seller-owned database schema and non-personal project data;
- transferable Seller-owned domains;
- transferable Seller-owned trademarks, names or logos;
- Seller-created project content;
- transferable app-store listing assets;
- project repositories and deployment configuration that do not expose excluded secrets; and
- other specifically identified project assets.

The Seller can assign only rights the Seller lawfully owns or is legally entitled to transfer.

## 5. Excluded Assets and LANERIQ Reserved Technology

The following are excluded unless a separate written instrument expressly grants them:

- the LANERIQ AI platform, brand, models, orchestration, internal services, algorithms, system prompts, proprietary libraries, shared templates, shared components, safety systems and infrastructure;
- LANERIQ AI source code not created solely as the Seller's project deliverable;
- LANERIQ AI provider accounts, private keys, API secrets, signing certificates, security rules, internal datasets and operational tooling;
- another customer's data, project, code or content;
- third-party model, hosting, app-store, payment, analytics or API accounts that are not transferable;
- open-source software except under its applicable licence;
- fonts, stock media, datasets, music and similar materials restricted by third-party terms; and
- any item marked **Excluded** in the Asset Schedule.

Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of LANERIQ AI Reserved Technology to the Seller or Buyer.

## 6. Completion Conditions

Completion occurs only after all conditions required for the transaction are satisfied, including as applicable:

1. identity/account verification reasonably required by the platform;
2. Seller authority and ownership declaration;
3. Buyer acceptance of the final Asset Schedule;
4. full cleared payment or approved payment-completion event;
5. electronic execution of this Agreement and required schedules;
6. third-party/open-source disclosure;
7. any required personal-data transfer assessment;
8. secure delivery of agreed assets or replacement credentials;
9. no active fraud, sanctions, chargeback, material IP, malware, security or ownership hold; and
10. issuance of the Handover & Acceptance Certificate.

LANERIQ AI may place a reasonable hold where evidence indicates fraud, unauthorized sale, infringement, compromised credentials, malware, unlawful data transfer, payment reversal, sanctions concern, account takeover or other material platform/security risk.

A hold preserves safety and evidence and is not itself a final legal determination of ownership.

## 7. Purchase price, fees and payment risk

The transaction record must distinguish:

- purchase price payable to the Seller;
- taxes, duties or withholding, if any;
- third-party processing charges; and
- any separate LANERIQ AI platform/transaction fee.

Unless mandatory law requires otherwise, a LANERIQ AI fee earned for completed document generation, transaction orchestration, verification or transfer-assistance services is not refundable merely because a party later changes its mind after the service has been performed.

A reversed or fraudulent payment does not create ownership in a party that failed to provide the agreed consideration. LANERIQ AI may freeze platform-side completion records where reasonably necessary to preserve evidence and reduce fraud risk.

## 8. Seller representations

The Seller represents at signing and Completion that, to the Seller's knowledge after reasonable inquiry:

1. the Seller has authority to transfer the listed assets;
2. known material third-party and open-source components have been disclosed;
3. the Seller has not knowingly inserted malware, credential theft, unauthorized surveillance or malicious backdoors;
4. the Seller has not knowingly concealed a material ownership dispute, injunction, lien or contractual restriction;
5. Seller-provided revenue, user, traffic and financial information is not intentionally false or materially misleading;
6. the Seller will not transfer personal data unlawfully; and
7. taxes and legal obligations attributable to the Seller remain the Seller's responsibility except where law or the final transaction record says otherwise.

## 9. Buyer due diligence

Before Completion, the Buyer is responsible for commercially reasonable due diligence appropriate to the transaction, including review of:

- functionality and known defects;
- source-code scope;
- third-party dependencies and recurring costs;
- open-source licences;
- app-store status;
- domain ownership;
- privacy/data obligations;
- infrastructure requirements;
- revenue/analytics evidence if relied upon; and
- the Asset Schedule and exclusions.

Except for fraud, intentional concealment, express written warranties, mandatory consumer rights and liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded, the Buyer accepts the Project in its documented condition at Completion.

## 10. No future-performance guarantee

To the maximum extent permitted by law, LANERIQ AI does not promise that:

- future app-store rules will remain unchanged;
- third-party APIs, AI providers or hosting will remain available or free;
- future costs will remain unchanged;
- generated code will never contain defects;
- the Project will achieve any revenue, valuation, ranking or growth outcome; or
- future updates will preserve all integrations.

## 11. IP assignment mechanics

The Seller assigns to the Buyer the Seller's assignable copyright and other transferable intellectual-property rights in the Transferred Assets to the extent expressly identified in the Asset Schedule and permitted by law.

The assignment does not extend to Excluded Assets or rights the Seller does not own.

Where a further filing, consent or signature is reasonably required to perfect an agreed transfer, each party must reasonably cooperate. The requesting party bears third-party filing/professional costs unless the transaction record states otherwise.

## 12. Third-party and open-source components

Material third-party or open-source components known to the Seller that affect transferability, attribution, source-disclosure obligations, commercial use, recurring fees or account ownership must be listed in the Third-Party & Open-Source Disclosure Schedule.

The Buyer takes those components subject to their applicable licences and provider terms. Neither Seller nor LANERIQ AI can transfer greater rights than the relevant third party granted.

## 13. Personal data and customer databases

Source code and personal data are treated separately.

No customer/user database is deemed transferred merely because application source code is sold. If personal data will move to or become controlled by the Buyer, the parties must complete the **LANERIQ AI App Sale Data Transfer Addendum** and any legally required notices, consents, security measures, controller/processor arrangements, retention/deletion steps, breach procedures and cross-border transfer requirements.

LANERIQ AI may block or delay platform-assisted personal-data export where required declarations are missing or a material legal/security risk is identified.

## 14. Confidentiality and credentials

Each party must protect non-public credentials, private keys, personal data, security information and confidential business information received during the transaction.

Secrets must never be placed in a public repository, screenshot, public certificate, public issue or publicly accessible transaction record.

Where feasible, Seller must revoke or rotate old credentials after Buyer establishes replacement credentials.

## 15. Indemnities protecting LANERIQ AI

To the maximum extent permitted by law, each Seller and Buyer is responsible for and shall indemnify the LANERIQ AI Operator and any lawful successor operator against third-party claims, losses, reasonable legal costs and liabilities arising from that party's:

- fraud, deliberate misconduct or material breach;
- infringement caused by material that party supplied or instructed the platform to use;
- unlawful personal-data processing or transfer under that party's control;
- taxes, duties or regulatory obligations legally attributable to that party;
- unauthorized sale or acquisition of assets;
- misuse of credentials, exported source code or acquired systems after handover; or
- violation of third-party licences or provider terms attributable to that party.

This clause does not shift liability for LANERIQ AI's own fraud, wilful misconduct or liability that applicable law makes non-excludable.

## 16. Limitation of LANERIQ AI liability

Subject to mandatory law, to the maximum extent legally permitted:

1. LANERIQ AI is not liable for indirect, consequential, special, exemplary or purely speculative losses, including projected profit, valuation, opportunity or goodwill;
2. LANERIQ AI is not liable for a third party's independent outage, policy change, account suspension or refusal to transfer an account;
3. LANERIQ AI is not responsible for undisclosed defects or ownership problems concealed by Seller and not independently verified by LANERIQ AI; and
4. aggregate liability of the LANERIQ AI Operator arising from platform transaction services for a particular App Sale is capped at the greater of the LANERIQ AI platform/transaction fees actually paid to LANERIQ AI for that specific sale or any minimum amount required by mandatory law.

Nothing excludes consumer rights, privacy obligations, fraud liability or other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

## 17. Evidence, disputes and electronic execution

Seller and Buyer should first use the platform evidence record and attempt good-faith resolution.

The final legally reviewed version is intended to use Malaysian law and a competent Malaysian forum except where mandatory law gives a party another right or forum.

The parties consent to electronic contracting. The execution system should record, as appropriate:

- authenticated user/account ID;
- signer legal name in the private execution record;
- exact Agreement Version and immutable hash;
- affirmative signature/acceptance action;
- signature timestamp;
- IP/session/device evidence proportionate to risk;
- OTP/MFA or equivalent signer-verification event for high-value transactions;
- project ID and transaction ID; and
- tamper-evident audit history.

A photographed handwritten signature is not required by LANERIQ AI's intended workflow and must not be stored in the public repository.

## 18. Current individual operator and future successor company

Until a lawful successor company transition becomes effective, the LANERIQ AI contracting operator is the individual identified in the private execution record, trading publicly as **LANERIQ AI**.

LANERIQ AI intends to begin company-transition readiness when the platform reaches approximately **1,000 registered users**. Reaching that threshold does not itself automatically substitute a new contracting party.

A successor company becomes operator only after, as applicable:

1. incorporation and verification of the successor company's legal identity;
2. qualified legal review;
3. assignment, accession, novation or other legally sufficient transition instrument;
4. preservation of existing customer/project rights and accrued obligations;
5. legally sufficient notice to affected users; and
6. affirmative consent where the law or relevant contract requires it.

The transition must not duplicate an existing project revenue share, cancel valid customer ownership, or create a second charge merely because LANERIQ AI changes operating entity.

This Agreement, the signed Asset Schedule, required addenda and the transaction record form the agreement for the sale scope they cover. If a provision is unlawful or unenforceable, it should be narrowed or severed only to the minimum extent necessary while preserving the remaining lawful allocation of risk.
