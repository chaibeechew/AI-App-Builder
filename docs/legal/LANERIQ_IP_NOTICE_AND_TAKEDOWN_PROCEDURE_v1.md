# LANERIQ AI Intellectual Property Notice & Takedown Procedure

**Version:** LANERIQ-IP-NOTICE-TAKEDOWN-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLIC ACTIVATION

This procedure is intended to govern copyright and other intellectual-property complaints relating to content, projects, listings or files hosted or made accessible through LANERIQ AI.

## 1. Purpose and scope

LANERIQ AI may provide hosting, build, preview, publishing, marketplace or storage functionality through which user-supplied or generated material becomes accessible. This procedure is designed to preserve evidence, respond proportionately to credible infringement notices, protect legitimate users from abusive complaints and support any applicable service-provider protections.

This procedure does not decide final ownership of disputed intellectual property. Courts, tribunals and rights owners remain responsible for final legal determinations.

## 2. Public designated contact requirement

Before LANERIQ AI relies on any statutory service-provider protection that requires a designated complaint contact, Production activation must publish legally sufficient contact information for the LANERIQ AI operator and a designated agent/contact for copyright notices.

The public contact record must not expose a residential address or private identity data unless legally necessary. If a publishable business/service address is legally required and no suitable address is available, the relevant safe-harbour feature remains NOT READY rather than publishing private residential information.

## 3. Required notice information

A claimant should provide enough information to permit a good-faith assessment, including:

- claimant name and contact details;
- identification of the copyrighted or other protected work;
- identification and location of the allegedly infringing material;
- explanation of the claimant's ownership or authority;
- a good-faith statement that the complained-of use is unauthorized where applicable;
- a statement that the information supplied is accurate to the claimant's knowledge; and
- an electronic or other legally sufficient signature/affirmation where required.

LANERIQ AI may request missing information before treating a notice as complete.

## 4. Initial response

Upon receiving a facially credible notice, LANERIQ AI may:

1. preserve the notice and relevant audit evidence;
2. restrict public access to the identified material where reasonably necessary;
3. notify the affected user unless prohibited by law or a legitimate safety/investigation need;
4. request additional evidence from either side; and
5. avoid deleting unrelated project content merely because one item is disputed.

Emergency restriction may be used for clear infringement, court orders, account takeover, malware, leaked credentials or other material legal/security risk.

## 5. Counter-notice / restoration

Where applicable law permits restoration after a counter-notice, settlement, court decision or other sufficient evidence, LANERIQ AI should maintain a documented process for the affected user to challenge the restriction.

Restoration must not occur merely because a user clicks a generic appeal button. The execution record should preserve the notice, counter-notice, timestamps, evidence and final action.

## 6. Repeat and abusive conduct

LANERIQ AI may suspend or restrict accounts that repeatedly infringe rights, knowingly submit false ownership claims, abuse the complaint process, sell projects they do not own or repeatedly evade prior restrictions.

A repeat-infringer policy must be applied proportionately and must not treat every disputed complaint as proven infringement.

## 7. Marketplace transactions

An active IP complaint, credible ownership dispute or court/regulatory hold may block App Sale completion, source-code handover, Buyout issuance or marketplace payout until the risk is resolved or sufficiently mitigated.

LANERIQ AI may preserve transaction evidence and prevent disposal of platform-side records during the hold.

## 8. Privacy and evidence minimization

Complaint records may contain personal data and confidential business information. Access must be limited to persons who need the information for legal, safety, support or dispute handling.

Private keys, passwords, complete identity documents and unnecessary personal data must not be included in public complaint records.

## 9. No admission

Removing, restricting, restoring or preserving material under this procedure is not an admission by LANERIQ AI that infringement did or did not occur.

## 10. Zero-cost current-stage implementation

At the current stage, LANERIQ AI may operate this procedure using existing authenticated accounts, support channels, audit logs and manual review without purchasing a specialist takedown platform. No paid legal workflow service is required merely to keep this draft procedure and evidence model ready.

If transaction scale, complaint volume or applicable law later requires specialist tooling, that migration must be separately approved.

## 11. Legal review gate

Before public Production activation, qualified Malaysian counsel should confirm the final notice fields, designated-agent/public-contact requirements, timelines, restoration/counter-notice mechanics, repeat-infringer policy and interaction with the Copyright Act 1987 and other applicable IP laws.
