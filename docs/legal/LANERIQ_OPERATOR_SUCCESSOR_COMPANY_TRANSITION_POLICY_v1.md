# LANERIQ AI Operator → Successor Company Transition Policy

**Version:** LANERIQ-OPERATOR-TRANSITION-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** INTERNAL LEGAL/OPERATIONS DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## 1. Current operating model

Until a successor company is lawfully incorporated and the transition becomes effective, LANERIQ AI contracts through the current individual Operator identified in the private contracting-party execution record and may trade publicly under the name **LANERIQ AI**.

The Operator's private legal name, personal address, signature specimen, government identification, banking details and private notice information must not be hard-coded into the public repository.

## 2. 1,000-user operational trigger

When LANERIQ AI reaches approximately **1,000 registered users**, the platform/business should begin the successor-company readiness process.

The 1,000-user threshold is an internal operational trigger only. It does not automatically create, substitute or novate a legal contracting party.

## 3. Required transition steps

Before the successor company becomes the contracting operator:

1. incorporate the successor company;
2. verify legal name, registration number and registered office;
3. establish required tax, payment and banking arrangements;
4. complete qualified legal review of existing platform agreements;
5. execute any required assignment, assumption, accession or novation instrument;
6. provide legally sufficient notice to affected customers/users;
7. obtain affirmative consent where applicable law or the relevant agreement requires it;
8. update public legal notices and private signing records;
9. update payment, invoicing, privacy/controller and support identities as legally required; and
10. preserve historical agreements exactly as originally executed.

## 4. Customer-protection continuity

The transition must not by itself:

- transfer ownership of a Creator's project to LANERIQ AI;
- duplicate or restart an existing revenue-share obligation;
- invalidate an existing valid Buyout;
- create an additional fee merely because the operating entity changes;
- silently change previously executed Agreement Versions; or
- erase accrued rights or liabilities.

## 5. LANERIQ-protection continuity

Subject to applicable law and properly executed transition documents, the successor company should receive the benefit of LANERIQ AI's existing platform rights, including accrued payment rights, licence restrictions, IP reservations, confidentiality protections, lawful liability limitations, indemnities and enforcement rights.

The individual Operator should be released from future obligations only to the extent legally effective under the final transition instrument; accrued rights and liabilities before the effective transition remain governed by applicable law and the executed documents.

## 6. Signing identity after transition

New agreements signed after the effective transition should identify the successor company as the LANERIQ AI contracting operator.

Existing agreements must retain the historical individual Operator identity in their private execution record. The platform must never rewrite historical signature evidence to make it appear that the successor company originally signed an agreement it did not sign.

## 7. Public/private separation

Public code and documents may state only that the current contracting operator is identified in a private execution record. Completed personal data must remain in private legal/signing infrastructure.

## 8. Legal review gate

A qualified Malaysian lawyer should approve the final successor-company transition mechanism, including Contracts Act, consumer, privacy, tax, payment, IP, assignment/novation and notice implications, before the company is substituted into Production contracts.
