# LANERIQ AI App Marketplace Terms

**Version:** LANERIQ-MARKETPLACE-v1-DRAFT  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION ACTIVATION

These Marketplace Terms govern listing, evaluating, buying and selling Apps or software projects through LANERIQ AI. They supplement the Platform Terms of Service.

## 1. Platform role

Unless LANERIQ AI expressly signs as a principal in a separate transaction document, LANERIQ AI provides marketplace software, transaction workflow, evidence records and transfer assistance and is not the Seller, Buyer, broker, fiduciary, trustee, investment adviser, legal adviser, tax adviser or guarantor of a project sale.

LANERIQ AI is not an escrow provider unless a separately approved regulated payment/escrow arrangement is activated.

## 2. Listing requirements

A Seller may list only a project the Seller is authorized to sell or license. The listing must accurately identify material facts known to the Seller, including as applicable:

- project identity and scope;
- included and excluded assets;
- source-code availability;
- material third-party/open-source dependencies;
- known recurring infrastructure/provider costs;
- app-store status;
- material known defects;
- material ownership restrictions or disputes;
- whether personal/customer data is proposed to be transferred; and
- financial or usage metrics only where the Seller can substantiate them.

LANERIQ AI may request evidence or remove a listing that appears fraudulent, unlawful, materially misleading, infringing, malicious or technically unsafe.

## 3. No guarantee of listing claims

Unless LANERIQ AI expressly labels a specific fact as independently verified, listing information originates from the Seller and must not be treated as a LANERIQ AI warranty.

Buyer remains responsible for commercially reasonable due diligence before Completion.

## 4. Due diligence access

A Seller may grant limited preview, demo or due-diligence access. Such access does not transfer ownership and does not authorize copying excluded source code, credentials, personal data or proprietary material beyond the agreed diligence scope.

Buyer must protect confidential information and may not use diligence access to compete unfairly, extract unrelated data or bypass the transaction process.

## 5. Offer, acceptance and final agreement

A listing is not necessarily a legally binding offer. The transaction becomes binding only through the final workflow specified for the transaction, including required agreement acceptance, payment event, asset schedule and signatures.

Where project ownership/IP is sold, the parties must electronically execute the LANERIQ App Sale & Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement and required schedules.

## 6. Payment and completion

Completion does not occur merely because a deposit is paid, a preview is viewed or one party clicks an initial interest button.

Unless the final transaction record lawfully states otherwise, Completion requires:

1. final price and currency confirmed;
2. required identity/authority checks completed;
3. final Asset Schedule accepted;
4. required agreements electronically signed;
5. cleared payment or approved payment-completion event;
6. required privacy/data-transfer conditions completed;
7. no active material fraud, malware, ownership or payment hold; and
8. Handover & Acceptance Certificate issued.

## 7. LANERIQ transaction holds

LANERIQ AI may place a reasonable temporary hold on listing, payment release, source delivery or transaction completion where evidence indicates:

- account takeover or identity mismatch;
- fraudulent or reversed payment;
- unauthorized asset sale;
- credible IP dispute;
- malware or malicious code;
- unlawful personal-data transfer;
- sanctions/legal restriction; or
- serious platform/security risk.

A hold preserves safety and evidence and is not a final determination of legal ownership.

## 8. Seller obligations

Seller is responsible for:

- authority to sell the listed assets;
- truthful material disclosures;
- lawful treatment of customer/end-user data;
- disclosure of known material third-party restrictions;
- secure removal or rotation of Seller-controlled credentials after handover;
- taxes and obligations legally attributable to Seller; and
- cooperation reasonably required to complete agreed transfers.

Seller must not include LANERIQ AI platform technology, provider credentials, other customers' data or other excluded assets in a sale.

## 9. Buyer obligations

Buyer is responsible for:

- reviewing the project and Asset Schedule;
- assessing code, business and infrastructure risks appropriate to the transaction;
- obtaining new provider, developer, hosting and payment accounts where required;
- complying with third-party licences and app-store terms after transfer;
- lawful post-transfer data processing; and
- taxes and obligations legally attributable to Buyer.

## 10. Personal data

Customer/end-user personal data is never presumed included merely because source code is sold.

Where personal data is proposed to move, the parties must satisfy the App Sale Data Transfer Addendum and applicable notice, consent, controller/processor, security, retention, breach and cross-border requirements before LANERIQ-assisted export.

## 11. Third-party accounts and licences

A project sale cannot transfer rights that a third party prohibits from transfer. App-store accounts, payment accounts, API/provider accounts, domains, licences, subscriptions, signing certificates and other services must be handled according to the relevant provider's transfer rules.

## 12. Fees

Any LANERIQ AI marketplace or transaction fee must be shown before the user commits to the paid transaction. The final Production pricing rules must clearly distinguish purchase price, LANERIQ fee, processor charge, tax and any revenue-share obligation.

## 13. Refunds, reversals and chargebacks

Refund eligibility is governed by the LANERIQ Refund, Cancellation & Chargeback Policy and mandatory law.

A Buyer must not use a chargeback as a substitute for good-faith dispute resolution after retaining transferred assets. LANERIQ AI may preserve evidence, freeze platform-side completion state, suspend related transaction privileges and seek recovery where a chargeback is fraudulent or abusive, subject to law.

## 14. Disputes between Seller and Buyer

Seller and Buyer should first use the transaction evidence record and platform dispute channel. LANERIQ AI may facilitate evidence exchange or temporary holds but does not become the legal decision-maker merely by operating the marketplace.

Nothing prevents a party from exercising mandatory legal rights or using a competent court/tribunal.

## 15. Intellectual-property disputes

LANERIQ AI may temporarily remove a listing or pause transfer after a credible IP complaint and may request evidence of ownership, licence or authority.

Knowingly false ownership statements or knowingly false infringement claims may result in account restriction and legal responsibility.

## 16. Risk allocation protecting LANERIQ AI

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Seller and Buyer are responsible for their own statements, due diligence, taxes, third-party licences, post-transfer operations and unlawful conduct.

LANERIQ AI does not guarantee future revenue, valuation, traffic, app-store approval, provider availability, defect-free software or continued commercial viability.

Business users are responsible for third-party claims arising from their fraud, infringement, unlawful data transfer, malicious code, unauthorized sale, misuse of acquired systems or material breach, subject to the limitations and non-excludable liabilities stated in the Platform Terms.

## 17. Suspension and marketplace removal

LANERIQ AI may restrict marketplace access for repeated fraud, misleading listings, unauthorized transfers, malware, unlawful data processing, repeated abusive chargebacks, material IP violations or evasion of prior enforcement.

## 18. Electronic signatures and evidence

High-risk sale and IP-transfer documents must use the LANERIQ Electronic Signature & Acceptance Evidence Standard. The intended record includes authenticated identity/account evidence, exact document version/hash, affirmative signing act, timestamp, project/transaction IDs and proportionate MFA/OTP controls.

Photographed handwritten signatures are not required for the standard workflow and must not be published in the public repository.

## 19. Operator transition

The current marketplace operator is the individual identified in the private legal record, trading as LANERIQ AI, until a lawful successor-company transition becomes effective.

The approximately 1,000-registered-user threshold is an internal readiness trigger only and does not automatically transfer existing agreements to a company.

## 20. Legal review gate

Before Production activation, qualified Malaysian counsel should review marketplace regulation, consumer protection, e-commerce, payments/escrow characterization, IP transfers, electronic signatures, tax/stamp duty, privacy/data transfers, dispute handling, limitation clauses and cross-border transactions.
