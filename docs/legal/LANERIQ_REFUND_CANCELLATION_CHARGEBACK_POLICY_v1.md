This Policy supplements the Platform Terms of Service and, where relevant, Marketplace Terms and project-sale agreements.

## 1. Mandatory rights preserved

Nothing in this Policy removes refund, cancellation, remedy or consumer rights that applicable law makes mandatory or non-excludable.

## 2. Before purchase

The Production checkout must clearly show the applicable product/service, price, currency, billing period, renewal rule, material limitations and any separately stated taxes or charges before the user commits to payment.

## 3. Subscription cancellation

A user may cancel future renewal of a subscription through the available account process. Unless mandatory law or a specific offer states otherwise, cancellation stops future renewal but does not retroactively unwind services already supplied during the current paid period.

Where technically feasible, paid access continues until the end of the already-paid period unless the account is terminated for serious breach, fraud, security abuse or another lawful reason.

## 4. Refund categories

Subject to mandatory law, refund review may distinguish among:

- duplicate or clearly erroneous charge;
- payment taken after a properly completed cancellation where renewal should not have occurred;
- material service failure attributable to LANERIQ AI where the purchased service was not substantially delivered;
- transaction cancelled before material paid work or irreversible delivery begins;
- App Sale transaction that fails a required Completion Condition before ownership transfer;
- fraud or account-compromise claim supported by appropriate evidence; and
- discretionary goodwill credit/refund approved by LANERIQ AI.

## 5. Generally non-refundable completed services

To the maximum extent permitted by law, fees for services already substantially performed are not refundable merely because a user changes their mind after receiving the service. This may include completed generation, export, verification, document-generation, transaction-orchestration, source-delivery or transfer-assistance work, where the checkout and applicable terms clearly disclosed the paid service.

This rule is subject to mandatory consumer rights and does not protect fraud, material misrepresentation or a material failure to provide the purchased service.

## 6. Buyout and project-specific licences

A Buyout or other project-specific licence must follow its specific agreement. Before activation, the final legally approved terms must specify whether and when the licence becomes irrevocably issued, what happens if payment reverses, and whether any statutory cancellation right applies.

No project-specific licence should be marked ACTIVE before the required payment and acceptance evidence is complete.

## 7. Marketplace/App Sale cancellations

Before Completion, a transaction may be cancelled according to the signed transaction terms and payment state.

After ownership/IP has validly transferred and the Buyer has received the agreed Transferred Assets, the transaction is not automatically reversible merely because the Buyer later changes preference, discovers an issue that was properly disclosed, or fails to complete the Buyer's own due diligence.

Fraud, intentional concealment, unauthorized sale, material breach, non-delivery and mandatory legal remedies remain separately reviewable.

## 8. Refund does not automatically reverse IP

For an App Sale, money reversal and IP ownership reversal are legally distinct. The platform must not silently mark IP ownership as reverted solely because a processor reports a refund or chargeback.

Where a completed sale is lawfully rescinded, the parties must follow a documented reversal process addressing source code, credentials, domains, app-store assets, customer data, copies/backups, confidentiality and any required reassignment document.

## 9. Chargebacks

Users should first use LANERIQ AI's support/dispute channel for a good-faith billing dispute where practical.

A chargeback must not be used fraudulently to retain delivered value while falsely claiming non-delivery or unauthorized use.

When a chargeback occurs, LANERIQ AI may, to the extent permitted by law:

- preserve payment, access, delivery and signature evidence;
- temporarily restrict the affected transaction or account;
- pause release of undelivered assets or funds;
- contest the chargeback with truthful evidence;
- recover an undisputed debt through lawful means; and
- require reasonable payment-risk verification before further high-risk transactions.

A chargeback alone is not proof that a user acted fraudulently.

## 10. Fraud and account compromise

LANERIQ AI may request proportionate evidence to distinguish unauthorized account use from buyer's remorse or a commercial dispute. High-risk claims may trigger credential reset, session revocation, payment hold and security investigation.

## 11. Processing times

The Production policy must state realistic review and processor settlement timeframes based on the actual payment provider. LANERIQ AI must not promise an exact bank posting time it does not control.

## 12. Payment processor rules

Refunds, reversals and chargebacks may also be governed by the applicable payment processor, bank or card-network rules. Those rules do not override mandatory law or the separate legal ownership analysis for transferred software/IP.

## 13. Abuse prevention

Repeated abusive refund demands, payment reversals after intentional consumption of paid services, use of multiple accounts to evade payment restrictions, or fabricated unauthorized-payment claims may result in risk controls or account restriction, subject to fair review and applicable law.

## 14. Records

LANERIQ AI may retain transaction, invoice, acceptance, delivery, security and dispute records for the period reasonably necessary for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, chargeback response, signed-contract evidence and legal claims, subject to the Privacy Notice and applicable law.
