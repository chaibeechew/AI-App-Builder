# Soolen AI App Store Revenue Tracking

## Commercial rule
- Creator Pass: US$10 per App, one-time.
- Free App license: held by the Soolen AI platform until the creator unlocks the App.
- After the Creator Pass is purchased, the creator receives the long-term license for that App.
- Soolen AI revenue share: 5% of revenue/proceeds attributable to selling the App itself through a supported app store, subject to the final Creator/Publishing Agreement and applicable store rules.
- Soolen AI takes 0% of the creator's independent products, goods, property, professional services, consulting, subscriptions, transaction fees, or other business revenue sold through the App.

## Apple
Use the official App Store Connect reporting/API mechanisms available to the platform account to reconcile App sales. Each published App must have a stable internal Soolen App ID mapped to the corresponding Apple identifiers/SKU/vendor identifier. Reconciliation should account for refunds, taxes, commissions, and the exact contractual revenue-share basis.

## Google Play
Use official Google Play Console reporting/API mechanisms available to the platform account to reconcile App sales. Each published App must have a stable internal Soolen App ID mapped to the corresponding Play identifiers and products. Reconciliation should account for refunds, taxes, service fees, and the exact contractual revenue-share basis.

## What is included in the 5%
Example: an App created with Soolen AI is sold for US$4.99. The qualifying App-sale proceeds are included in the 5% calculation.

## What is excluded
Example: the App is used by a real-estate company to sell a US$300,000 property. That property sale is the company's independent business revenue and is excluded from Soolen AI's 5% share.

## Admin records
Store and reconcile:
- Soolen App ID
- Creator/user ID
- License status
- Apple App ID / SKU / vendor identifier where applicable
- Google Play identifiers where applicable
- Reporting period
- App-sale units
- App-sale proceeds
- refunds/adjustments
- qualifying revenue-share base
- Soolen AI 5% share
- creator statement status

Never estimate store revenue from app-open counts or advertisements. Use official store reporting for settlement.
